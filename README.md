# Uttarakhand Landslide Route Safety Web App

## Project Overview

This project is a web-based application designed to help drivers travelling on hilly roads in Uttarakhand make safer travel decisions. Uttarakhand is highly prone to landslides, road blockages, sudden weather changes, and route disruptions, especially during the monsoon season.

The application provides route-specific weather updates, AI-assisted risk information, and a reporting system where drivers can report problems they observe on the road. It also helps local officials monitor vulnerable routes and share important updates with the public.

## Problem Statement

Drivers travelling through hilly regions often face unexpected risks such as landslides, heavy rainfall, road cracks, falling rocks, and blocked routes. In many cases, drivers do not receive real-time or route-specific information before starting their journey.

Local officials also need a simple platform to monitor route conditions and collect reports from people travelling through affected areas.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a web app focused on landslide-prone routes in Uttarakhand. It allows drivers to check the safety condition of a route before travelling and report any road-related problems they encounter.

The system provides AI-assisted risk analysis by combining route information, weather data, and user reports. Based on this information, the app can indicate whether a route is safe, risky, or requires caution.

## Target Users

### 1. Drivers
Drivers can use the app to:
- Check route-specific weather updates
- View landslide or road blockage risk information
- Find safer travel options
- Report problems such as landslides, road cracks, fallen trees, or blocked roads
- Make better travel decisions before and during the journey

### 2. Local Officials
Local officials can use the app to:
- Monitor vulnerable routes
- View reports submitted by drivers
- Identify high-risk locations
- Share alerts or safety updates
- Take faster action during emergencies

## Key Features

- Route-based weather updates
- AI-assisted landslide and route risk analysis
- Driver problem reporting system
- Map-based route monitoring
- Risk levels for routes such as Safe, Moderate Risk, and High Risk
- Local official dashboard for monitoring reports
- Alerts and updates for vulnerable routes
- Simple and user-friendly interface

## How It Works

1. A driver enters or selects a travel route.
2. The app fetches route-related weather and condition information.
3. The AI system analyzes possible risk based on weather, terrain, and reports.
4. The app displays a safety status for the selected route.
5. Drivers can report any issue they notice while travelling.
6. Officials can view these reports and share updates or warnings.

## Example Use Case

A driver planning to travel from Dehradun to Joshimath can check the route on the web app before starting the journey. If heavy rainfall or previous landslide reports are detected on the route, the app can show a warning and suggest caution. If another driver reports a road blockage, officials can verify the report and update the route status for other users.

## Main Goal

The main goal of this project is to improve road safety in landslide-prone areas of Uttarakhand by providing timely, route-specific, and easy-to-understand risk information to drivers and officials.

## Benefits

- Helps drivers avoid dangerous routes
- Reduces risk of accidents in hilly areas
- Improves communication between travellers and officials
- Enables faster reporting of road problems
- Supports better disaster response and route monitoring
- Encourages safer travel planning in landslide-prone regions
